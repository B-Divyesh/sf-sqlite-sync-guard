Terms and safety limits

SQLite Sync Guard is free software under the MIT License. These notes explain its limits.

Keep another backup

Keep a tested backup before moving database files or changing sync settings. A safe scan describes only the moment it ran.

Do not use two writers

The tool does not make writes from two synced computers safe. It does not merge data or repair existing corruption.

No warranty

The software and site are provided “as is,” without warranty, as far as the law permits. The source repository includes the full license.
